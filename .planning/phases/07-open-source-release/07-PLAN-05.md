## Overview

`agent-energy-bridge` is a sidecar service that sits next to your LLM gateway (e.g. `sub2api`, `new-api`, `one-api`, OpenAI-compatible gateways). Instead of replacing your gateway, it complements it with agent-first capabilities:

- Model capability recommendation
- Low-balance refuel and activation-code redemption
- Onboarding doc generation
- Shadow routing suggestions
